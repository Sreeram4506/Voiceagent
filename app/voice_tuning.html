How It Works

IndicF5 is a 330M-parameter flow-matching TTS model from AI4Bharat, trained on 1417 hours of Indian language speech.

Zero-shot voice cloning: Give it a 3-10 second reference audio + transcript, and it generates speech in that voice.

Supported languages: Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Gujarati, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Assamese.

Pipeline

Caller → Whisper STT → GPT-4o → IndicF5 TTS → Caller
